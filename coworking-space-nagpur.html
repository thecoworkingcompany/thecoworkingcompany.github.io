Questions.

How much does a coworking seat cost in Nagpur at TCC?
Most seats are ₹7,000–8,000 per seat, per month, with electricity, high-speed leased-line internet, UPS and DG power backup, parking, and tea and coffee included. Final pricing depends on branch, layout and term.
Can I try the space before joining?
Yes. A day pass is ₹699 and includes a desk for the day and one combo meal at BREAK, our café. You can also book a free tour.
Which areas of Nagpur are your branches in?
Dharampeth (C Square, Mata Mandir Road, Khare Town), Dev Nagar (JK Heights, Ajni Square) and Orange City Mall 1 in Somalwada, which has three branches and BREAK café.
Is there an app for members?
Yes. Members book meeting rooms, invite guests, order from BREAK, raise requests and see invoices in the TCC member app on Android and the web.
