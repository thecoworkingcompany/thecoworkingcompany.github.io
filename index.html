      Before you sign anywhere

      Six questions to ask
      any workspace provider.

      The monthly price is only part of the story. Ask these wherever you look, and compare the answers.

      Is electricity part of the fee?

      At TCC, yes. Electricity is included with every workspace.

      What happens when the power goes?

      UPS plus DG backup, so calls and servers keep running.

      Is parking extra?

      No. Car and two-wheeler parking come with the workspace.

      Can we see our meeting-room hours before we book?

      Yes. The TCC app shows your included hours before you confirm, not on the invoice.

      Can the space be built around our team?

      Yes. From a branded cabin to a fully managed Headquarters floor, configured for your IT, storage and processes.

      Who answers when something breaks?

      An onsite team, and every request is tracked in the app from received to resolved.

      Questions

      Before you visit.

      What's included in the price?

      Electricity, high-speed leased-line internet, UPS and DG power backup, parking and complimentary tea and coffee by CCD come with your workspace. There are no add-on charges for the basics.

      Which workspaces can we choose from?

      Orbit, a dedicated single workstation; The Cabin, a private cabin for small teams; The Constellation, a private workspace for 8–10 people; and The Headquarters, a floor or section designed, built and run around your business. Meeting rooms, virtual office addresses and custom company sections are also available.

      Where are your branches?

      Five branches in Nagpur: Dharampeth (C Square, Mata Mandir Road), Dev Nagar (JK Heights, Ajni Square) and three branches at Orange City Mall 1, Somalwada, where BREAK café is on the 4th floor.

      Can the space be customised for our team?

      Yes. We start with how your team works and configure layouts, IT, storage and branding around it. The Headquarters option is fully designed and managed for you.

      How do members book rooms and raise requests?

      Through the TCC member app: book meeting rooms with your included hours, invite guests, order from BREAK, raise requests and see invoices. Members sign in with the email their company registered with us.

      How do we book a tour?

      Use the form below, call +91 80870 50221 or +91 94230 33341, or message us on WhatsApp. We'll recommend the right space before you visit.
