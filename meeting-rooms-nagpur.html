Questions.

How many people do your meeting rooms seat?
Our rooms seat 4, 6, 8 and 12 people. The 12-seat boardroom and 8-seat conference room are at Orange City Mall 1.
Can non-members book a meeting room?
Meeting rooms are for members. Call +91 80870 50221 to ask about a membership or a one-off booking.
How do members book?
In the TCC member app: pick a room, a date and a time. Included hours are used first; extra time is billed on the monthly invoice.
